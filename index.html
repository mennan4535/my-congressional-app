Primary Emergency Contacts

Every household should set up three primary emergency contacts

Details

When a life-threatening situation occurs, 911 is the immediate response. Emergency contacts provide the personal, medical, and legal support that follow.

Examples

  • A primary family member or guardian
  • A trusted local friend or relative
  • An out-of-town contact

Emergency Guidelines

Cardiac Arrest

Step 1: Tap person and speak loudly. If unresponsive and not breathing, call 911 and get an AED.

Step 2: Lay flat on their back on a firm surface.

Step 3: Perform CPR compressions in the center of the chest (100–120 per minute).

Burns

Step 1: Move away from heat source.

Step 2: Run cool tap water over burn for 10–20 mins (never ice).

Step 3: Cover loosely with clean gauze or plastic wrap.

Building on Fire

Step 1: Crawl low under smoke toward exits.

Step 2: Check doors with the back of your hand before opening.

Step 3: Evacuate using stairs (never elevators).

Pocket Guide: PAS Protocol

  • A - Alert: Call 911 / 112 immediately.
  • S - Succor / Save: Provide basic first aid.
